Dataset: StudentsPerformance.csv (Kaggle: spscientist/students-performance-in-exams)
Rows: 1000 student records
Columns:
1) gender: student gender category (female/male)
2) race/ethnicity: group label (A, B, C, D, E)
3) parental level of education: parent education background
4) lunch: lunch type (standard, free/reduced)
5) test preparation course: completed or none
6) math score: integer score out of 100
7) reading score: integer score out of 100
8) writing score: integer score out of 100

Exp6 Analysis Query Set
Simple queries:
- GenderCount: count students by gender.
- LunchTypeCount: count students by lunch type.
- HighestMathByRace: max math score per race/ethnicity group.

Intermediate queries:
- AvgScoresByGender: average math, reading, writing by gender.
- AvgTotalByParentalEducation: average total score (math+reading+writing) by parental education.
- TestPrepImpact: average total score by test prep completion status.

Complex queries:
- PerformanceBandDistribution: classify each student into performance bands using average score (Excellent/Good/Average/Poor).
- TopNStudentsByTotalScore: global Top-5 student records by total score using descending sort in reducer.
- ScoreGapBucketCount: bucketized distribution of reading-math gap to analyze comparative subject strength.
- DistinctScoreTriplesCount: count distinct (math,reading,writing) combinations across all students.

Output Artifacts
- output/: raw job outputs
- verification/: extracted part-r-00000 results for each query
- output_ss/: PNG snapshots generated from verification files
